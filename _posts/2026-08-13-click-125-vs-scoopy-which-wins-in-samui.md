![Which is better Click 125 vs Scoopy for city riding on Samui?](https://samui-bikes.com/images/honda-click-125-vs-honda-scoopy-city-comparison.webp)

I rented the Scoopy for half a week and the Click 125 for the other half in Chaweng last March, same rider and route, to settle it. Weekly cost: the Scoopy's 300 baht a day is 2,100, the Click 125's 180 is 1,260 - an 840 baht saving that covers a beachside dinner or two days of attraction fees. On the same town loop the Click 125 returned 48 km/l against the Scoopy's 40, though that is only about 56 baht across the week. On the ring road north of Mae Nam the Scoopy's suspension soaked up the concrete joins the Click 125 passed straight to the seat - irrelevant over 20 km, real over 50. But through the roundabout the Click 125, 40 mm narrower and 8 kg lighter, was always easier to place. Choose the Click 125 for town riding and a tighter budget, the Scoopy for ring road distance, gradients or all-day comfort.
