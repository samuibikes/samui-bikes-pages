![Which is better Z650RS vs Royal Enfield on Samui?](https://samui-bikes.com/images/kawasaki-z650rs-vs-royal-enfield-samui.webp)

I rode the Z650RS and Himalayan 450 on back-to-back mornings over the same ring road circuit and the Nathon plantation track, and they are complementary opposites. On the ring road the Z650RS cruised 90 km/h with its twin relaxed while the Himalayan worked its single harder at 75; at the plantation track it reversed, the Himalayan riding all 7 km while the Z650RS turned back at the gravel entrance. On tarmac the Z650RS wins decisively - 67 relaxed horsepower, modern suspension, ABS and traction control against the Himalayan's buzzier, higher-revving single. Off-road the Himalayan wins decisively on 230 mm clearance, a 21-inch front and adventure tyres the Z650RS's road setup cannot match. Both draw attention from different audiences and rent within 150 baht of each other. Any plantation track needs the Himalayan; an all-tarmac trip the Z650RS, for highway pace, looks and 67 relaxed horsepower for touring.
