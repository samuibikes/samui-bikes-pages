![When Honda Click 125 is the smart budget choice on Samui?](https://samui-bikes.com/images/honda-click-125-affordable-rental-price-board.webp)

I spent seven days in Chaweng last November using a Click 125 for everything - markets, beach, evening restaurant runs - and covered 140 km on 95 baht of fuel; the whole week's transport came to 1,355 baht, less than one Bangkok airport taxi. That is the case for this scooter. Its natural ceiling is about 50 km of ring road or 30 km of mixed town riding a day, and on a 32 km Chaweng-Big Buddha loop it never showed effort at over 48 km/l. Where budget is the constraint, the 150 baht a day it saves over the Scoopy compounds into a snorkelling trip or a cooking class across ten days. In the Chaweng roundabout at peak hour its 96 kg and narrow profile stop and restart in less width than the NMAX beside it. Pick it when your day stays under 40 km in the beach corridor and cost beats performance; add distance or hills and step up.
