![Who is Honda Click 125 best for on Koh Samui?](https://samui-bikes.com/images/honda-click-125-beginner-couple-samui.webp)

At the Chaweng rental strip last February I watched a first-time British rider pick the Click 125 over the Scoopy on the owner's advice - lighter, lower, easier to park on narrow beach lanes - and she came back two hours later to extend for a second day. That is the pattern the Click 125 produces. It is usually the cheapest scooter in the fleet, 150 to 200 baht a day under the Scoopy, saving 1,000 to 1,400 baht across a week. At 96 kg dry with a 760 mm seat you flat-foot at stops, and the gentle throttle never lunges when a nervous hand over-twists. Two Australians rode the Chaweng-to-Lamai beach circuit three days running under 20 km a day, a tankful lasting four days. It is not for ring road speed, hills or long pillion runs. It is best for the first-time or occasional rider around Chaweng or Lamai who values simplicity and low cost.
